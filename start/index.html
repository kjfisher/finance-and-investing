Students: how a class day works

1 · Warm-upScan the QR code on the board (or open Warm-Ups) and answer.
2 · NotesOpen today's guided notes in Notability, or grab a printed copy.
3 · PracticeSlides, a case study, a game in the Arcade or the D214 Investing Competition.
4 · Exit ticketScan the second QR code, answer, and show your result screen on the way out.

Where things live

  • Daily Agenda: today's plan. Tap Today, and pick your blocks (1 & 3 or 5 & 7) so the dates match.
  • Unit pages (from the agenda or any lesson): slides, notes, case studies, portfolio pieces and practice games.
  • Readings: one reading per unit, with a guide (regular or Supported) and a 5-question check.
  • Glossary: every key word, with flashcards. My Targets: rate yourself on each learning target and get relearn links.

Every paper you turn in

Write Name (first name + last initial) · Block · Caddy # at the top of every page. Everything comes as a PDF for Notability and as a printed copy.

Grading and reassessment

Each unit quiz is 2 pages and scored out of 20 (17 one-point questions + a 3-point claim-evidence-reasoning answer). Scores convert to a standards-based score:

ScoreUnit quiz (of 20)Final exam (of 50)
1.018–2045–50
0.8516–17.540–44.5
0.7014–15.535–39.5
0.5513.5 or less34.5 or less
  1. Below 1.0? Complete the unit's Reassessment Ticket (fix your errors, relearn, practice).
  2. Get it signed by your teacher.
  3. Take Version B. Your higher score counts.

Tip: use My Targets to find which targets to relearn before you start the ticket. Supported versions of every quiz and reading guide have larger print and extra scaffolds.

Families

This course follows the investment process professionals use: set goals, learn how markets work, analyze companies and funds, build a diversified portfolio, manage risk, and avoid scams and bad decisions. Students trade in the D214 Investing Competition with simulated money only. No real money is invested.

Easy ways to help at home

  • Ask which stock or fund your student picked in the competition, and why.
  • Read one of the unit readings together. Each one takes about 10 minutes.
  • Share a money goal from your own life and how you planned for it (Unit 1 is all about goals).
  • Ask your student to teach you one word from the glossary.

Questions about grades or progress: please contact the teacher through the school's usual channels.

Substitute teachers

  1. Open the Daily Agenda, pick the class's blocks, and tap Today.
  2. Open that day's lesson page (link on the agenda card). It has the learning target and a timed plan: what students do in each part of the block.
  3. Project the slides with ▶ Today's slides. Arrow keys move slides. Students follow along in their guided notes.
  4. Start with the Warm-up QR code on the agenda card and end with the Exit ticket QR code (students show their result screen).
  5. Need a backup plan? Use the unit's practice games in the Arcade (Team Showdown works for the whole class), or have students do that unit's reading guide from Readings.

Assessments and answer keys are not on this site. Leave quizzes for the teacher unless you were given them directly.
