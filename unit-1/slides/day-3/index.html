How money moves · Notes box 2

Savers → intermediaries → borrowers

SaversFamilies and workers with money they don't need today
IntermediariesBanks, brokers, mutual funds, insurers: pool savers' money
BorrowersBusinesses, governments and families that need money now
Why it mattersSavers earn interest or profits; borrowers build, hire and grow
How money moves · Notes box 2

The money loop

Savers lend or invest; borrowers build and hire; interest and profits come back.

Notes box 3

Primary vs. secondary market

Primary market

A company sells NEW shares or bonds, for example in an IPO. The company gets the money.

Secondary market

Investors trade existing shares with each other, for example on the NYSE or Nasdaq. The company gets nothing.

The trade-off

More risk, more expected return

  • Investors expect extra return for taking extra risk
  • Expected is not guaranteed: risky assets can lose a lot
  • A promise of high return with no risk is a red flag
