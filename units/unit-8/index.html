Key vocabulary

BlockchainA shared record of transactions, copied on many computers, grouped into blocks linked in order.
HashA digital fingerprint of a block. Each block stores the previous block's hash, linking the chain.
MiningComputers compete to add the next block and earn new bitcoin. Bitcoin's supply is capped at 21 million.
Wallet / private keyYour private key proves you own your coins. Lose it and you lose access. “Not your keys, not your coins.”
StablecoinCrypto designed to stay near a set value, like $1.
Bitcoin ETFA fund that trades like a stock and tracks bitcoin's price.
Red flagsGuaranteed returns, pressure to act fast, anonymous founders, pay to recruit friends, locked withdrawals.
Rug pullCreators of a token vanish with investors' money.
Rental cash flowRent − (mortgage + taxes and insurance + repairs and other costs).
REITA company that owns income property; trades like a stock; must pay out most taxable income as dividends.
LiquidityHow fast you can turn something into cash without losing value.
CommoditiesRaw goods like oil, wheat and gold. Gold pays no income; its price moves with fear, inflation, rates and the dollar.
CollectiblesSneakers, cards, art: no income, hard to sell, fees and fakes.
Startup valuationInvestment ÷ stake. $500,000 for 20% → $2,500,000.
