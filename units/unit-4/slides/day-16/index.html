Notes box 2

Roth vs. Traditional

Roth (IRA or 401(k))

Pay tax now. Growth and retirement withdrawals are tax-free.

Traditional (IRA or 401(k))

Tax break now. Pay tax when you withdraw in retirement.

Notes box 3

Capital gains

Held 1 year or lessShort-term: taxed like your paycheck
Held more than 1 yearLong-term: lower rates (0%, 15% or 20% for most people)
DividendsMany are taxed at the lower long-term rates
Fund distributionsTaxed each year in a regular account, even if you didn't sell
Inside a Roth or 401(k)No tax on trades or dividends each year
