Standard deviation

Small standard deviation

Returns stay close to the average. A calmer ride.

Large standard deviation

Returns swing far above and below. A bumpier ride.

Standard deviation, step by step

Average(0 − 5 + 7 + 6) ÷ 4 = 2%

Notes box 3

Two kinds of risk

Systematic (market)

Recessions, rate hikes, pandemics. Hits almost everything. Can't diversify it away.

Unsystematic (company)

A scandal, a recall, a failed product. Hits one company. Diversify it away.

Beta

Beta works both ways

A beta of 1.5 means bigger gains in good years AND bigger losses in bad ones.

Risk-adjusted return

The Sharpe ratio: reward per unit of risk

Formula(return − T-bill rate) ÷ standard deviation
Stocks, 1928–2025(11.9% − 3.4%) ÷ 19.4% ≈ 0.43
10-year Treasury bonds(4.8% − 3.4%) ÷ 7.9% ≈ 0.18
Use itHigher = more reward for each unit of risk taken
