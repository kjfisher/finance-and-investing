Bid, ask, spread · Notes box 2

Bid $24.90 · Ask $25.00

BidThe most a buyer will pay: $24.90
AskThe least a seller will take: $25.00
Spread$25.00 − $24.90 = $0.10

Where your order fills

Buy at market → you pay the ask. Sell at market → you get the bid.

Order types · Notes box 3

Market, limit, stop

Market orderBuy or sell now at the best price · always fills
Limit orderOnly at your price or better · may never fill
Stop orderBecomes a market order at your stop · limits a loss
Margin · Notes box 4

$5,000 of yours + $5,000 borrowed buys $10,000 of stock

Stock rises 20%$12,000 − $5,000 owed = $7,000 → +40% for you
Stock falls 20%$8,000 − $5,000 owed = $3,000 → −40% for you
The lessonBorrowed money doubles both the gains and the losses

Short selling · Notes box 4

Betting a price will fall

If you're right

Sell borrowed shares at $50, buy them back at $40: +$10 a share.

If you're wrong

Buy them back at $70: −$20 a share. Prices can keep rising, so losses have no upper limit.
