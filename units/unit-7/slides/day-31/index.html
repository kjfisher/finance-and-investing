Call payoff · Notes box 2

$50 strike, $3 premium

At expiration · strike $50 · premium $3 a share

At or below $50 you lose the $3 premium. Breakeven is $53. Above that, every $1 the stock rises adds $1 of profit.

Put payoff

$40 strike, $2 premium

At expiration · strike $40 · premium $2 a share

A put gains as the price FALLS below $38. The most the buyer can lose is the $2 premium.

Notes box 3

Two basic strategies

Covered call

Own 100 shares + SELL a call. Earn the premium; give up gains above the strike.

Protective put

Own the shares + BUY a put. Insurance against a drop; the premium is the cost.
