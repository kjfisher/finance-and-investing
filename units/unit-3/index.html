Key vocabulary

Business modelHow a company makes money.
MoatA lasting advantage that protects profits: brand, network effect, low cost or switching costs.
Fundamental vs. technical analysisFundamental: study the business and its numbers. Technical: study price charts and patterns.
Revenue / net incomeRevenue: money from sales. Net income: what's left after every cost and tax.
Profit marginNet income ÷ revenue.
EPSEarnings per share = net income ÷ shares outstanding.
Balance sheetA snapshot of what a company owns and owes on one day: assets = liabilities + equity.
Debt-to-equityLiabilities ÷ equity. Higher = more borrowed money, more risk.
Current ratioCurrent assets ÷ current liabilities. Above 1 = can pay short-term bills.
Free cash flowCash from operations − spending on property and equipment.
10-KThe annual report every public company files with the SEC.
P/EPrice ÷ EPS: what you pay for each $1 of yearly earnings.
PEGP/E ÷ expected growth rate. Near 1 = price fits the growth.
Dividend yieldYearly dividend ÷ price.
