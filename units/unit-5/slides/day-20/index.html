Notes box 2

Stock vs. bond

Stock

You OWN part of a company. No promised payments.

Bond

You LEND money. The issuer promises interest and your money back.

Worked example · Notes box 2

$1,000 bond · 4% coupon · 10 years · paid twice a year

Interest each year$1,000 × 4% = $40
Each payment (every 6 months)$40 ÷ 2 = $20
Total interest over 10 years$40 × 10 = $400
At maturityYou get the $1,000 back
