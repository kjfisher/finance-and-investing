Key vocabulary

Event dayMonday, April 5 · all blocks · in person: rounds in the D214 simulator, then group Portfolio Defense with judges.
Scoring100 points. Judges score REASONING, not portfolio value. 70 points come from 7 specific categories.
Notes sheetYou may bring it into the judge room, but only if it's filled out.
Group rolesIndicator watcher · current-events watcher · trader/portfolio tracker.
Rule of thumbNo more than 40% in one sector; at least one defensive holding (bonds or consumer staples).
Analyst biasRaj: bonds + government spending · Eleanor: energy · Dave “The Bull”: bold, volatile sectors · Maria: low rates drive everything.
Sector recapHistorical context for a hypothesis, not a guarantee.
