Key vocabulary

Real assetA physical thing that is useful or produces something: a house, farmland, gold, a factory.
Financial assetA claim on money or on a company's future profits: a stock, a bond, a fund share, a bank deposit.
Compound interestInterest on your interest. Each year's growth is added to the balance and grows too.
Rule of 72Years to double ≈ 72 ÷ the yearly rate.
Inflation / CPIPrices rising over time / the Consumer Price Index that measures it.
Real returnYour return minus inflation. It can be negative.
Financial intermediaryA bank, broker, fund or insurer that connects savers with borrowers.
Primary vs. secondary marketPrimary: a company sells new shares (an IPO) and gets the money. Secondary: investors trade with each other.
Asset classA group of similar investments: cash, bonds, stocks, funds, real estate, commodities, derivatives, crypto.
GDPThe total value of goods and services a country produces.
Unemployment rateThe share of people who want a job but don't have one.
Fed funds rateThe interest rate banks charge each other overnight; the Federal Reserve sets its target.
Business cycleExpansion → peak → contraction → trough, then back to expansion.
