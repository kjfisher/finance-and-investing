Case Studies

Real events, real numbers: 5 cases for every unit (40 in all). Each is about 20 minutes, with a Supported version.

Try the Case Lab simulators: all 8 unit cases ↗

For teachers: ways to use the library

  • Case of the Week: every Market Monday (schedule below), about 15–20 minutes, or assign it as homework.
  • Flexible: early finishers, sub plans, homework, reassessment practice, or a jigsaw (groups take different cases from the same unit and teach each other).
  • Each case: a short reading, 2 math or reading questions, 2 short answers and a 3-point “Your Call” claim-evidence-reasoning answer. Keys are in your F&I project.
