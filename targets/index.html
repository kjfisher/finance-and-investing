My Targets

Every learning target for Units 1–8. Rate yourself after each lesson and before each quiz. Your ratings stay on this device only: nothing is sent to your teacher or graded.

How to use it

  1. After a lesson, tap how well you know each target.
  2. Anything at 1 or 2 shows Relearn links: the lesson, a practice game and the unit's glossary.
  3. Quiz score below 1.0? Bring your Focus list to your Reassessment Ticket conference.
